About us

A multi-dimensional
company.

RCS International is a premier, newly established multi-disciplinary contractor headquartered in the strategically vital hub of Kismayo, Somalia. Formed by a group of regional and international logistics, construction, and corporate operations experts, we serve as a single-point-of-contact partner for government agencies, international defense entities, NGOs, and private firms across the Jubaland and Somalia region.

We bridge the gap between rigid international compliance frameworks and the ground realities of field execution in East Africa. Using modern project management methodologies, direct local supply channels, and a completely vetted network of local human capital, we execute projects safely, transparently, and exactly on schedule.
