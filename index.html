Our services

Built around
your success.

Six services under one oversight model, for government agencies, international defense entities, NGOs and private firms.

01

Construction

RCS delivers certified, professional construction and property solutions designed to give you complete peace of mind. Specializing in comprehensive structural and system coverage, our expert team manages everything from initial planning to rigorous quality inspections across residential, commercial, and multi-unit projects.

02

Logistics and Transportation

From navigating complex international freight channels to managing regional door-to-door distribution, RCS optimizes your entire supply chain. By prioritizing cost-effectiveness, stringent safety compliance, and end-to-end monitoring, we eliminate operational disruptions and ensure your cargo reaches its destination seamlessly.

03

Procurement

From specialized power equipment (such as diesel generators, transformers, and solar/inverter systems) to real estate and agricultural assets, RCS acts as a trusted third-party Procurement Management partner, with rigorous vendor evaluation, market research, and end-to-end execution to ensure absolute transparency.

04

Warehousing

Through strict vendor evaluation and third-party oversight, RCS helps small, medium, and large organizations secure facilities that feature real-time inventory tracking, rigorous quality control, and pristine safety standards, aligned with your broader procurement timelines.

05

Freight and Customs Clearance

By thoroughly auditing documentation, including HS code classification, country-of-origin validation, and import/export permits, RCS minimizes the risk of port delays, fines, or unexpected storage fees. We coordinate with certified customs brokers and global carriers across air, sea, and land routes.

06

Labour Workforce

Through rigorous third-party assessment and workforce compliance tracking, RCS ensures your labor force is aligned with your operational timelines. From technical staff and administrative personnel to project-based laborers, we help you source the right talent with strict standards for efficiency and risk management.

About us

A multi-dimensional
company.

RCS International is a premier, newly established multi-disciplinary contractor headquartered in the strategically vital hub of Kismayo, Somalia. Formed by a group of regional and international logistics, construction, and corporate operations experts, we serve as a single-point-of-contact partner for government agencies, international defense entities, NGOs, and private firms across the Jubaland and Somalia region.

We bridge the gap between rigid international compliance frameworks and the ground realities of field execution in East Africa. Using modern project management methodologies, direct local supply channels, and a completely vetted network of local human capital, we execute projects safely, transparently, and exactly on schedule.
